# Variety-selection division in language revival — research record (2026-11-15)

## Verified damage cases (sources fetched this session)

- **Lakota / LLC** — Rosebud Sioux Resolution 2012-343 (tribe adopted White Hat orthography; banned LLC's "Czech orthography" from the reservation); Standing Rock banished the organization 2022-05-03; Acoma ended its contract 2019. NBC News (Graham Lee Brewer), 2022-06-03: https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/native-american-language-preservation-rcna31396 — Cheyenne River 2018: "we spend too much time arguing about the orthography… not using it." Attribution: outsider-held standard + orthography competition; NOT dialect favoritism (LLC dictionary was dialect-inclusive).
- **Cornish** — KK adoption by vote (1987) "caused a division in the Cornish language community"; two decades of attack literature; "no single orthography had ever achieved a wide consensus" → 2008 SWF. Post-SWF output stagnated (2025 study). sources: Wikipedia Cornish language revival + Kernewek Kemmyn; Davies-Deacon, Language Problems & Language Planning 44:1 (2020), https://benjamins.com/catalog/lplp.00056.dav
- **Breton** — Wmffre, *Breton Orthographies and Dialects Vol. 2: The Twentieth-Century Orthography War in Brittany* (Peter Lang, 2007), https://www.peterlang.com/document/1043735; Hewitt ch. in Jones (ed.), CUP 2015 (DOI 10.1017/9781316562949.011)
- **Norwegian målstrid** — Wikipedia Norwegian language conflict (fetched; state-scale analog, generations of conflict, no "failure").
- **Scholarly anchors** — Dorian 1994, Language in Society 23(4):479–494 (purism vs compromise; abstract verified); Hinton 2014 via Lakota Wikipedia (writing-system choice "a serious point of contention… fraught with political as well as pedagogical complications").

## Verified positives (projects that did not fracture)

- **Mohawk 1993** six-nation consensus standardization; "Standardisation does not mean the elimination of dialects" (Lazore report, full text: https://mohawklanguageca.wordpress.com/2021/04/21/the-mohawk-language-standardisation-project/)
- **Wôpanâak** — intertribal MOU governance (Mashpee/Aquinnah/Assonet/Herring Pond), https://www.wlrp.org/project-history
- **Mutsun** — per-entry selection guidance with provenance notes ("So use taprey… so use uuni instead"), OCR text: https://archive.org/download/20240528_20240528_1934/MutsunDictionary_WarnerButlerGeary_djvu.txt
- **palawa kani** — recorder-fidelity + statewide meetings, https://tacinc.com.au/programs/palawa-kani/
- **Cornish SWF** — consensus-instead-of-victory, variants of equal standing, displaced forms recorded, scheduled 2013 review

## Lead corrections (do not repeat)

- Mohawk "disputes": none documented — inverse (consensus success).
- Anishinaabemowin Odawa-vs-Ojibwe dialect-damage: nothing citable found.
- SNEA-specific damage: none found; pattern is cooperative (WLRP; Fielding's Mohegan-Pequot-Montauk single-language dictionary).
- Lakota "favored one tribe's dialect": not the documented axis; the documented axis is contested standardization AUTHORITY (+ outsider control, data sovereignty).

## Synthesis

Documented damage attaches to **contested authority over choice-making without shared reviewable machinery** — tribal bans, banishments, decades of stagnation are real. "Selecting a variety → failure" as a universal is NOT supported; the positive cases make selection acceptable exactly by procedure (stated criteria, recorded alternants, named review, scheduled review). For the draft: a corrected-framing blurb is supportable; a "selection will fail the project" claim is not.
