The Brothertown Language project is a public resource for the revival of the
language of the Brothertown Indian Nation. Every dictionary entry and lesson
text on this site will be generated from the research repositories' validated
data files, never hand-typed onto pages.

This about page is part of the base site setup; fuller biography, method, and
reference material arrive in later releases.
