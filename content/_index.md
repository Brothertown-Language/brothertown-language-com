The Brothertown Language project documents, teaches, and shares the language
of the Brothertown Indian Nation. This site will carry the dictionary, written
and audio lessons, and apps as they are built.

This first version is the site skeleton — the dictionary, lessons, apps, and
methodology sections arrive in later releases.
